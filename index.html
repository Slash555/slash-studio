SLASH STUDIO is a personal, locally operated video production tool. Its YouTube integration is designed to let the operator authorize their channels and upload finished videos from the desktop workflow.

01 / CREATE

Local workflow

Organize production stages, video assets and publishing readiness in one workspace.

02 / CONNECT

YouTube authorization

Connect a YouTube channel with Google OAuth. Google account passwords are never requested by the application.

03 / PUBLISH

Controlled uploads

Upload videos and metadata to an authorized YouTube channel, with confirmation of the intended channel.

How YouTube data is used

With the account holder's permission, the application requests youtube.upload to upload videos and thumbnails and youtube.readonly to identify and inspect the authorized channel. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, or unrelated Google services.

Personal-use integration. SLASH STUDIO is operated locally by its owner. The public pages explain the application's purpose and data handling; they are not a hosted replacement for the desktop production tool.

Account control and transparency

Google OAuth permissions are granted per YouTube connection. Access can be revoked from your Google Account settings. Read the Privacy Policy for details on data use, local storage, and deletion.
