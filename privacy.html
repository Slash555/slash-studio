Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026 · Application: SLASH STUDIO

This policy explains the intended handling of YouTube and Google account data when the operator uses the locally running SLASH STUDIO video production application.

1. Application and operator

SLASH STUDIO is a personal Windows-based video production and YouTube publishing tool. It is used to connect YouTube channels that the operator owns or is authorized to manage. Questions about this policy can be sent to airballoooon55@gmail.com.

2. Information accessed

When the operator explicitly authorizes a YouTube channel, the application may access the following via Google OAuth and YouTube Data API v3:

  • YouTube channel identity and basic channel information needed to identify the intended upload destination (youtube.readonly).
  • Authorization tokens for the selected YouTube connection, used to access authorized API features without collecting the account password.
  • Video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags, and other upload information provided by the operator for publishing (youtube.upload).
  • API responses needed to confirm upload status, video identifiers, and errors.

The application does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive documents, or unrelated Google account data.

3. How information is used

Data is used only to identify authorized channels, prepare and upload the operator's selected video content, display upload results, and maintain the authorized connection. Each connected channel must be deliberately selected; authorization to one channel is not intended to authorize uploads to a different channel.

Google user data is not used for advertising, sale, third-party marketing, or training general-purpose AI models.

4. Storage and sharing

SLASH STUDIO is intended to operate locally on the user's computer. The YouTube integration is designed to store its OAuth connection information and production records in the operator-controlled local environment, rather than in a developer-operated cloud database. Authorized video content and metadata are transmitted to Google's YouTube services when an upload is initiated.

This website is a static information site and does not contain an upload form, tracking pixels, advertising scripts, or an analytics service. Its hosting provider may maintain ordinary technical access logs under that provider's own privacy policy.

The application does not intentionally share Google user data with external advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated services.

5. Retention and deletion

Local production records and OAuth connection information are retained as needed for the operator's workflow, until the connection or the relevant local data is deleted. The account holder can revoke OAuth access at any time through Google Account third-party access settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized API requests but does not, by itself, erase copies of files already saved locally.

To request help locating and deleting locally stored account credentials or related data, contact airballoooon55@gmail.com. Credentials should also be removed from the local computer when a channel is disconnected. No centralized database of connected Google accounts is intended to be maintained by the application developer.

6. Security

Google OAuth authorization is used instead of asking for Google passwords. OAuth files and tokens must be protected on the operator's device and must not be published in source repositories or public websites. Access to the desktop environment should be limited to authorized users. No software can guarantee absolute security.

7. Google API Services and YouTube

SLASH STUDIO's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the application may be reviewed and revoked at Google Account permissions.

8. Changes to this policy

Changes to the application or its data handling practices may require updates to this policy. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made. Where authorization scopes change, the operator will be asked to grant any necessary additional permissions.

9. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data deletion assistance, email airballoooon55@gmail.com.
